Next Stop

Live Singapore bus arrivals on a 3D map

Next Stop shows every bus arriving at the stops around you, glides each bus along its real route on a 3D map of Singapore, and keeps your bus on the Lock Screen while you wait or ride.

Get help

Found a problem or have an idea? Open an issue and include the bus stop code and service number if it's about a specific bus. We read every report.

Common questions

Why are no buses showing?
Next Stop uses live data from the Land Transport Authority. Buses don't run late at night, and arrival times disappear when a service has ended for the day. Outside Singapore, pan the map to Singapore to see stops.

What does Next Stop Pro include?
Live Activities on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, drop-off alerts before your stop, live rain and cloud on the map, and unlimited Bus Uncle. Without Pro, Bus Uncle is free for an hour a day.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?
Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. To restore it on a new device, open Settings in Next Stop and tap Become a Pro Commuter, then Restore Purchases.
